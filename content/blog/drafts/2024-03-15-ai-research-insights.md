## The Research-Production Gap

One of the biggest challenges in AI development is bridging the gap between research and production. Research environments are controlled, datasets are clean, and computational resources are abundant. Production is messy, data is noisy, and efficiency matters.

### Key Challenges

1. **Data Distribution Shift**: Real-world data rarely matches training data
2. **Computational Constraints**: Research models are often too large for production
3. **Latency Requirements**: Academic benchmarks don't account for real-time constraints
